# Landing page (frontend/app/page.tsx)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: hookathon judges and Uniswap v4 LPs. Action: understand, then open the app. Beliefs the first screen must land: I can make any rule; it runs while I'm away; my money is safe. Feels wrong: generic crypto look (dark neon, gradients, glass, stat rows), playful or gimmicky. Rejected earlier: poster sketches, night-sky scroll story, playing-card hand with suits, sentence builder, draggable chart lines, building blocks.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Alucard is the movement inside your position: wind it once with your rules and it keeps working when you put it down. Refuses the DeFi landing default (dark hero, gradient glow, stat row, feature cards).

OWN-WORLD: A watchmaker's technical catalogue: cool white paper, graphite ink linework, one ruby red for jewels and the moment a rule acts, hairline gilt for engraving. The movement is drawn, not rendered: bridges with Geneva stripes, a wheel train, a balance wheel and hairspring, ruby jewels, blued screws. Labels set like engraving: small spaced capitals.

STORY: The visitor sees a working movement whose bridges name the parts of a rule; scrolls and the watch turns over to a dial that runs one rule on a backtest of real Chainlink prices (understand first: the user's primary action is to understand, then open the app); then chooses complications in the configurator (any condition, any action, repeats, limits) with a crown on the dial; learns the case is sealed (money stays in Uniswap, the contract enforces every limit, anyone can wind it, no one can open it); sees the certificate of tests; opens the app. Amended after the finish review: the replay precedes the configurator for that reason.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left, headline "Wind it once. It keeps working." with one paragraph and two actions (Open the app, primary; How it works). Right, at 560px, the live movement: wheels turning at their true ratios, the balance oscillating, ruby jewels at the pivots, engraved labels on the bridges naming the parts of a rule (When, Then, Limits, Keeper). Nothing else.

FORM: Impeccable's pick (my rank 1 of 7, the automatic watch movement), seed: 70f19e5f8cd77c3d. Signature interaction: the configurator, where turning the crown sets a complication's value and its hand sweeps to it. Motion grammar: mechanical only; continuous wheel rotation at gear ratios, balance oscillation, hands that sweep with a spring and settle; scroll turns the watch over from movement to dial. Raises kept: one time scrubber drives the replay (paper automata); plain words reveal on-chain fields on demand (teletext REVEAL); one object per viewport (fashion flagship).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
