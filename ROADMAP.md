# Roadmap

- **Move action:** exit a position and re-add it to another listed pool (cross-pool) or the same pool (re-range, compound) in one transaction.
- **Round trip:** move to a stable pool on a drop, move back on a recovery.
- **Pool APR condition:** fire when the pool's fee APR goes above or below a level, measured on-chain.
- **Alerts:** Telegram or email when a position goes out of range or an order runs.
- **Backtest across pools:** add swap history for destination pools.
- **AI strategy builder:** describe a strategy in words, get a rule set ready to backtest.
- **Split the contract:** under 24 KB for mainnet.
- **Loans for LPs?:** since LP's can also trade we can give them more trade options like take a loan but somehow use this amount from a blackbox which can be in any pool in any ratio and be used to lend money right into the protocol.
